Privacy Policy

Vero Financial Ltd · Company No. 16652225 · ICO Ref: ZC084647

Vero Financial Ltd (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is a company registered in England and Wales (company number 16652225). We are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy and to providing clear, transparent information about how your personal data is used.

We are the data controller for the personal data we collect through our website and mobile application. We are registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), registration reference: ZC084647.

Definitions

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, Personal Data means any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual. This includes, for example, your name, email address, account identifiers, online identifiers, and information you choose to provide when using the Services.

Collection of personal data

When you use the Services, we collect and process personal data that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Account details (such as email address and username)
  • Expenses and financial information you manually enter or, if you choose to turn on Auto-tracking, that Auto-tracking logs for you
  • Receipts you choose to scan or upload
  • Details of Gift Cards you buy through Vero
  • Budget preferences and goals
  • Gamification and progress data
  • Communications with our support team

We do not access your bank accounts, collect bank credentials, or retrieve data from third-party financial institutions in the MVP version of Vero. Auto-tracking, if you turn it on, reads notifications on your device. It does not connect to your bank (see “Auto-tracking (optional)” below).

What data we collect

We collect only the data necessary to operate the Service and deliver its features.

a) Account information

  • Email address
  • Username or display name
  • Encrypted password
  • User ID

b) User-entered financial data

  • Expenses you manually input (amounts, categories, dates, optional notes)
  • Transaction details taken from notifications on your device, only if you turn on Auto-tracking
  • Budget goals and preferences
  • AI-generated insights based solely on the data you provide
  • Gamification data (XP, streaks, challenges, progress)

c) Technical & usage data

  • IP address
  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • Log files and error reports
  • Basic usage analytics to improve app performance

d) Communications

  • Messages you send to our support team
  • Email preferences and communication history

e) Receipts and Gift Card purchases

  • Receipt images and the details read from them, if you scan or upload receipts
  • Details of Gift Card purchases (such as the Brand, amount, discount applied and date), and your plan (Free or Plus) at the time
  • We do not receive or store your payment card or bank details for Gift Card purchases (see “Gift Cards and sharing data with SpendStream” below)

If you choose to upload images (for example, attaching a receipt to an expense or adding a profile picture), we will store and process those images solely to provide the relevant feature. Uploading images is optional.

Auto-tracking (optional)

Auto-tracking is an optional feature that logs your transactions for you. It is off unless you turn it on. Vero does not require it, and you can use Vero fully by entering expenses manually.

If you turn it on, you give Vero permission to access notifications on your device so that we can identify and log transactions automatically. We use this access only to log your transactions and provide the Service.

Auto-tracking only works while notifications are turned on. How you turn it on depends on your device:

  • iPhone (iOS): you set it up through the Shortcuts app settings on your iPhone.
  • Android: you allow notification access inside the Vero app.

Auto-tracking does not give us access to your bank account or banking login details, and we cannot make payments or move money.

You can turn Auto-tracking off at any time: on iPhone, through the Shortcuts app settings; on Android, inside the Vero app. From then on we stop accessing those notifications. You can also delete logged transactions, or your whole account, at any time.

Receipt scanning and camera access (optional)

Receipt scanning is optional. If you choose to scan a receipt, Vero asks for permission to use your device’s camera, and we use the camera only while you are using that feature. You can say no, or withdraw the permission at any time in your device settings, and the rest of Vero will keep working. You can always enter expenses manually instead.

When you scan a receipt, we read details from it, such as the amount, merchant and date, to create an expense for you. Receipts can contain personal details, so only scan receipts you are happy for us to process.

Gift Cards and sharing data with SpendStream

If you buy a Gift Card through Vero, we work with SpendStream Ltd (company number 16056707), which supplies the Gift Cards and processes your payment.

What we share. We share your Vero user ID, your plan (Free or Plus) and details of your purchase. SpendStream uses them to apply your Instant Discount, take payment, issue the Gift Card and match the purchase to your account.

Payment. You pay SpendStream directly through its secure payment page. We never see or store your card or bank details. SpendStream may collect the payment and contact details it needs to complete your purchase.

SpendStream’s role. SpendStream handles this data for us. It is also a separate controller for its own fraud-prevention, security, anti-money-laundering, sanctions-screening, payment, billing, legal-compliance, and service analytics and improvement purposes, which means it decides how to use data for those purposes under its own privacy notice. It may keep transaction records for up to six years for accounting and legal reasons. It may transfer data outside the UK where necessary, using approved safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

We do not sell your personal data, including to SpendStream or any brand partner.

Use of your personal data

We use the personal data you provide to:

  • Operate and deliver the Services
  • Enable expense tracking and budgeting, manually or through Auto-tracking if you turn it on
  • Read receipts you choose to scan, to create expenses for you
  • Process Gift Card purchases and apply your Instant Discount, including by sharing the data described above with SpendStream
  • Generate AI-powered insights based on your inputs
  • Provide gamified features that support positive financial habits
  • Maintain the security and performance of the Services
  • Communicate with you about your account or support requests

The information we collect is used only for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy and in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

We process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Contract – to provide the Service you sign up for (including Gift Card purchases)
  • Legitimate interests – to secure, maintain, and improve the Service (and to prevent fraud and misuse)
  • Consent – for optional communications such as marketing emails (where applicable) and, if you turn them on, for Auto-tracking and for camera access when you scan receipts

You may withdraw consent at any time (including by turning Auto-tracking off).

Automated processing & insights

Vero uses behavioural insights, challenges, rewards, and visual feedback to encourage positive financial habits. These features:

  • Are informational and motivational only
  • Do not guarantee specific financial outcomes
  • Should not be relied upon as professional advice

How long we keep your data

  • Your personal data is stored while your account remains active
  • You may delete your account at any time
  • Once deleted, your personal data is permanently erased or anonymised within a reasonable timeframe
  • Technical logs are retained only as long as necessary for security and diagnostics
  • If you turn Auto-tracking off, we stop collecting notification data from your device
  • SpendStream may keep Gift Card transaction records for up to six years for accounting and legal reasons

Who we share data with

We share your data with SpendStream, our third-party Gift Card provider, and with our brand partners. We do not sell your personal data to anyone, including SpendStream or our brand partners. We may also share limited data with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate Vero. The third parties we share data with include:

  • Cloud hosting providers
  • Email and notification services
  • Analytics and error-monitoring tools
  • App stores (Apple App Store, Google Play), which process your subscription payments. If you request a refund from an app store, we may tell it about your subscription and your use of it
  • SpendStream Ltd, which supplies Gift Cards and processes Gift Card payments (see “Gift Cards and sharing data with SpendStream” above)
  • Our brand partners, so they can provide the discounts and offers you use through Vero

All service providers are bound by contractual obligations to protect your data and process it only on our instructions (except where a provider is also a separate controller for its own legal purposes, as SpendStream is, see above).

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on secure servers located in the United Kingdom and/or European Economic Area (EEA). If data is transferred outside these regions in the future, appropriate safeguards will be applied in accordance with data protection laws. SpendStream may transfer data it holds outside the UK where necessary, using approved safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete your data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent (where applicable)
  • Object to certain processing activities

You can exercise these rights through in-app settings or by contacting us. We respond to requests within 30 days. For data that SpendStream holds as its own controller, you can also contact SpendStream through its privacy notice.

Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS)
  • Secure password hashing
  • Access controls and monitoring
  • Regular system updates and security reviews

No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to safeguard your information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If changes are material, we will notify you through the app or by email. The latest version will always be available on our website.

Contact us

If you have any questions, concerns, or complaints about the Service, or if you would like to get in touch with us, please contact our support team at vero.fincorp@gmail.com.
