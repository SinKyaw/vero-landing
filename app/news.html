Vero News

Tokens are more valuable now

Our herd is growing! So many of you have joined Vero recently, and just as many rewards have been redeemed.

To keep that momentum sustainable for everyone as we scale, we’re making a few changes to how tokens work.

What changed:

Vero Tokens take a little more consistent effort to earn!

Tracking your expenses will give you only 1 token now, so if you want to get 2 per day — you got to check that budget daily.

XP matters more than ever!

We’re introducing leagues — tiers based on how active you are. Only the top leagues earns tokens, so start stacking that XP and climb your way out of the Silver Calf League!

Remember!

Good habits are built slowly, not instantly. Making tokens a little harder to earn means the reward means more when it arrives, and the habit sticks longer than the payout does 🐘
