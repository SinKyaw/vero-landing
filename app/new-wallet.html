Meet your new Wallet

You told us you wanted more ways to manage your money with Vero, so we built a set of features to help you organise your finances, plan ahead and see the bigger picture — all in one place.

Wallet has two parts:

Manage your money

Add your account balances, organise money into pockets (and share them with friends), set savings goals, plan for upcoming bills, and create spending plans for your week, month or next trip.

See the bigger picture

Build your own dashboard with the charts and insights that matter most to you. Choose what you want to see, all in one place.

What happens to the Vero you know?

Nothing changes! Your money tracking, Ask Verdi, current budget and monthly challenge are all staying right where they are.

Soon, new challenges and rewards will be built around what you set up in your Wallet. Start exploring now, and you’ll be ready when they land.

Ready to explore?

Open your Wallet, make it your own, and help us shape what comes next.
