Vero vs YNAB

By the Vero team•Updated 24 November 2026•4 min read

We make Vero, one of the apps in this guide. Nobody paid to be included, and we’ve tried to be fair: every app gets the same questions, and we say where others do things better. Prices and features checked on 24 November 2026. Spot something out of date? Email vero.fincorp@gmail.com.

Short answer

  • YNAB is a thorough zero-based budgeting app. You plan where every pound goes before you spend it. It costs $14.99 a month or $109 a year, after a 34-day free trial.
  • Vero is lighter. You set aside your fixed costs, then watch one safe-to-spend number each day. It’s free, with Plus at £4.99 a month.

Pick YNAB if you want maximum control and enjoy planning. Pick Vero if you want something quick that you’ll keep using.

The real difference: the method

YNAB is built on zero-based budgeting. When money comes in, you assign all of it to categories until nothing is left unplanned. When you overspend, you move money from another category to cover it.

Vero asks for less. You set your payday, put money aside in pockets for rent, bills and savings, and Vero works out what’s safe to spend each day until you’re paid again.

Zero-based budgeting gives you a precise plan, and many people swear by it. It also takes regular attention. A single daily number is less detailed, but it’s easier to check in a few seconds.

Effort and learning curve

YNAB has a lot to learn, and it knows it: there are guides, videos and workshops to help you. Once it clicks, users often say it changes how they think about money.

Vero is designed to take seconds a day. You log a spend by texting Verdi, or let Plus log it from your notifications. Challenges and streaks give you a reason to come back.

Price

YNAB costs $14.99 a month or $109 a year, plus tax where it applies. There’s no free plan, but the 34-day trial doesn’t need a card. Vero is free, and Vero Plus is £4.99 a month.

YNAB lists prices in US dollars, so what you pay in pounds depends on the exchange rate and your card’s fees.

When YNAB is the better choice

  • You want to plan every pound and enjoy that level of detail.
  • You’re happy to spend time learning a method.
  • You want the option of linking UK bank accounts.

When Vero is the better choice

  • You’ve tried detailed budgets before and stopped using them.
  • You want a free plan, or to pay in pounds.
  • You want motivation from challenges, streaks and rewards.
  • You never want an app connected to your bank.

Questions people ask

Does YNAB work in the UK?

Yes. You can set it to show pounds, enter transactions by hand, or link select UK banks. Prices are charged in US dollars.

Is there a free version of YNAB?

No. YNAB offers a 34-day free trial with no card needed, then it’s $14.99 a month or $109 a year.

What is zero-based budgeting?

It means planning where all your income goes, so that income minus everything you’ve planned equals zero. Savings count as a plan too.

What’s a safe-to-spend number?

It’s what you can spend today without dipping into money set aside for bills, rent or savings. Vero works it out from your plan and what you’ve already spent.
