A lighter YNAB alternative,free and in pounds

By the Vero team•Updated 24 November 2026•6 min read

Short answer

YNAB is one of the most thorough budgeting apps there is. If its method works for you, it’s worth the price: $14.99 a month or $109 a year, after a 34-day free trial.

Vero is lighter. You budget as loosely or as tightly as you like, logging takes seconds, and streaks and challenges help you keep going. It’s free, with Plus at £4.99 a month.

Pick YNAB if you enjoy planning every pound. Pick Vero if you’ve tried a detailed budget before and stopped.

What YNAB does well

  • A proven method. Zero-based budgeting gives every pound a job before you spend it. Users often say it changed how they think about money.
  • It teaches you. YNAB has guides, videos and workshops to help the method click.
  • Bank links if you want them. In the UK you can link select banks, or enter everything by hand.

If you use YNAB every week and it works, keep it. This page is for people it hasn’t stuck with.

A method to keep up vs a habit that keeps you going

YNAB asks a lot and gives a lot back. Every time money comes in, you assign it. Every time you overspend, you move money to cover it. The plan is only as good as the attention you give it.

If you’ve ever given up on a detailed budget, the method probably wasn’t the problem. Keeping it up was.

Vero starts from the other end. It makes the daily part quick enough to stick, then lets you add as much planning as you want. Cap a few categories and leave it there, or give money to pockets for rent, bills and savings, much like YNAB’s categories.

How Vero works instead

  • Plan it your way. Make a plan for the month, the week or a stretch like a trip or a term. Use category caps, pockets or both. Money you save is kept apart, so it never shows up as money to spend.
  • Know what’s free to use. Add your accounts by hand and Vero shows what’s safe to spend: money that doesn’t have a job yet, after bills still due.
  • Log in one sentence. Type or say what you spent, or snap a receipt. On iPhone, Siri and the Action button work without opening the app. On Android, Vero can log your banking app’s payment notifications.
  • Keep it going. Streaks, challenges and weekly leagues earn XP and tokens, and tokens bring back discounts on gift cards from Verdi’s trips.
  • Take your data with you. Export everything to an Excel workbook with live formulas, or your transactions to CSV.

Price

YNAB costs $14.99 a month or $109 a year, plus tax where it applies. There’s no free plan, but the 34-day trial doesn’t need a card. Vero is free, and Vero Plus is £4.99 a month. Budgeting is the same on both Vero plans.

YNAB lists prices in US dollars, so what you pay in pounds depends on the exchange rate and your card’s fees.

Moving from YNAB

Vero doesn’t import from YNAB, so you start fresh. That’s less work than it sounds: add your accounts with today’s balances, set up a plan for this month, and log from today. Your YNAB history stays in YNAB.

You could keep YNAB for the plan and use Vero only for the daily habit, but you’d be entering spending twice. For most people, one place to log is easier.

Which one fits you

When YNAB is the better choice

  • You want to plan every pound and enjoy that level of detail.
  • You’re happy to spend time learning a method.
  • You want the option of linking UK bank accounts.

When Vero is the better choice

  • You’ve tried detailed budgets before and stopped using them.
  • You want a free plan, or to pay in pounds.
  • You want motivation from challenges, streaks and rewards.
  • You never want an app connected to your bank.

Questions people ask

Is there anything YNAB does that Vero doesn’t?

Yes. YNAB teaches a complete budgeting method, with guides, videos and workshops, and it can link select UK banks. Vero doesn’t teach a single method and never links to a bank.

Does YNAB work in the UK?

Yes. You can set it to show pounds, enter transactions by hand, or link select UK banks. Prices are charged in US dollars.

Is there a free version of YNAB?

No. YNAB offers a 34-day free trial with no card needed, then it’s $14.99 a month or $109 a year.

Can I give every pound a job in Vero?

Yes, if you want to. Put money into pockets for each purpose, such as rent, bills and savings, from the accounts it sits in. Or keep it light with a few category caps. One plan can mix both.

What does “safe to spend” mean in Vero?

It’s the money in your accounts that you haven’t given a job yet, minus bills still due that their own pockets don’t cover.
