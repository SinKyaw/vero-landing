Vero vs Emma

By the Vero team•Updated 24 November 2026•4 min read

We make Vero, one of the apps in this guide. Nobody paid to be included, and we’ve tried to be fair: every app gets the same questions, and we say where others do things better. Prices and features checked on 24 November 2026. Spot something out of date? Email vero.fincorp@gmail.com.

Short answer

  • Emma connects to your bank accounts through open banking and gives you one automatic view of your money.
  • Vero never connects to your bank. You log spending in seconds, plan your month, and earn rewards for good habits.

Pick Emma if you have several accounts and want everything in one place automatically. Pick Vero if you want to build a spending habit without sharing bank access.

How each app gets your numbers

This is the real choice between the two. Emma links to your bank with your permission, so transactions appear by themselves. You see balances and spending across accounts without typing anything.

Vero never links to your bank. You tell it what you spent, or on Plus it reads your payment notifications. That takes a little more effort, but it shares less and makes each spend more noticeable.

Features compared

Budgeting

Emma’s budgets, which let you set monthly limits and track categories over time, start on its Plus plan. The free Basic plan focuses on seeing your accounts.

Vero’s monthly plan is free. You set it around your payday, put money aside in pockets for rent and bills, and get a safe-to-spend number each day.

Overview of your money

This is where Emma is stronger. Linking several accounts gives you one place to see your balances and spending. Vero doesn’t show balances, because it never sees your bank.

Motivation

Vero is built to make tracking a habit. Challenges, streaks and leagues earn tokens, which unlock discounted gift cards. Emma doesn’t list challenges or rewards on its plan comparison.

When Emma is the better choice

  • You have several bank accounts or cards and want one view of all of them.
  • You’re comfortable with open banking and want tracking to be automatic.
  • You want yearly billing, or a 7-day free trial before you pay.

When Vero is the better choice

  • You don’t want to connect your bank to any app.
  • You want budgeting on a free plan.
  • You track cash or shared spending that a bank feed would miss.
  • You want challenges and rewards to keep you going.

Questions people ask

Does Emma use open banking?

Yes. Emma says it uses open banking to give real-time updates from your connected accounts.

Is Emma free?

Emma has a free Basic plan. Budgeting, more bank logins and other features are on paid plans, starting with Plus at £4.99 a month or £41.99 a year.

Can Vero see my bank balance?

No. Vero never connects to your bank, so it can’t see balances or transactions. It only knows the spending you log, or what your payment notifications say on Vero Plus.

Can I use both?

Yes. Some people use Emma for a full overview of their accounts and Vero for daily logging, challenges and rewards.
