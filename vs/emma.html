An Emma alternativethat doesn’t need open banking

By the Vero team•Updated 24 November 2026•6 min read

Short answer

Emma is a strong choice if you want one automatic view of all your accounts. It does that through open banking, and Vero doesn’t try to compete with it.

Vero is for building a daily spending habit without connecting your bank. You log in seconds, plan your month and earn rewards for sticking with it. Budgeting is free.

Pick Emma for the overview. Pick Vero for the habit. You can also use both.

What Emma does well

Emma is popular in the UK for good reasons:

  • One view of everything. Link your accounts and see balances and spending in one place, updated automatically.
  • No typing. Transactions appear by themselves, so nothing gets forgotten.
  • Plans for different needs. A free Basic plan, then paid plans from £4.99 a month, with yearly billing and a 7-day free trial.

If you have several accounts and want them all in one place without effort, Emma does that better than Vero.

A dashboard vs a daily habit

Emma is a dashboard. It gathers your money in one place so you can look whenever you like. That works well if looking is enough.

Vero is a routine. Instead of a feed, you log each spend in a sentence as it happens, reflect on the ones that matter, and keep a streak going. It’s more effort than a dashboard, on purpose: noticing a spend is the first step to changing it.

How Vero works instead

  • Log in one sentence. Type or say “coffee 3.20 and 12 for lunch at Pret” and Vero files both spends, with amount, place and category. You can also snap a receipt.
  • Log without opening the app. On iPhone, use Siri, the Action button or a Shortcut. On Android, Vero can offer to log the payment notifications from banking apps you choose. It reads the notification, never your bank account.
  • Plan it your way. Make a plan for the month, the week or a trip. Cap a few categories, or give money to pockets for rent, bills and savings. Budgeting is free, and it’s the same on Plus.
  • Add your accounts by hand. Vero can hold your bank, cash, savings and card accounts, with balances you enter, and show what’s safe to spend once bills are covered. It never connects to them.
  • Keep it going. Streaks, daily and weekly challenges and weekly leagues earn XP and tokens. Tokens send Verdi, Vero’s elephant, on trips that bring back discounts on gift cards from brands you already use.

Honestly, you can keep both

Emma and Vero do different jobs, so they sit well side by side. Use Emma to see every account in one place. Use Vero for the part a dashboard can’t do: noticing each spend, asking why, and sticking to a plan.

One tip if you use both: keep your budget in one app. Two budgets for the same money gets confusing fast.

Which one fits you

When Emma is the better choice

  • You have several bank accounts or cards and want one view of all of them.
  • You’re comfortable with open banking and want tracking to be automatic.
  • You want yearly billing, or a 7-day free trial before you pay.

When Vero is the better choice

  • You don’t want to connect your bank to any app.
  • You want budgeting on a free plan.
  • You spend cash, or pay for friends and family, and want it logged the way it really is.
  • You want challenges and rewards to keep you going.

Questions people ask

Is there anything Emma does that Vero doesn’t?

Yes. Emma connects to your bank accounts and shows your balances and transactions automatically, in one place. Vero can hold your accounts too, but you enter the balances and spending yourself.

Does Emma use open banking?

Yes. Emma says it uses open banking to give real-time updates from your connected accounts.

Is Emma free?

Emma has a free Basic plan. Budgeting, more bank logins and other features are on paid plans, starting with Plus at £4.99 a month or £41.99 a year.

Can Vero see my bank balance?

No. Vero never connects to your bank, so it can’t see your balances or transactions. If you add an account to Vero, you type in the balance yourself.

Can I use both?

Yes. Some people use Emma for a full view of their accounts and Vero for the daily habit: logging, reflecting, challenges and rewards. If you do, keep your budget in one app so you aren’t tracking the same money twice.
