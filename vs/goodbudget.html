A Goodbudget alternative,priced in pounds

By the Vero team•Updated 24 November 2026•6 min read

Short answer

Goodbudget is a solid, long-running envelope budgeting app with a genuinely free plan. If you like planning every envelope, it does that well.

In the UK, though, its bank sync doesn’t work, so you type in every transaction. Vero is built around making that part quick: one sentence, your voice or a receipt, with streaks and challenges to keep you going.

Pick Goodbudget if you want a web app and a classic envelope method. Pick Vero if typing everything in is where your budget keeps falling apart.

What Goodbudget does well

  • A clear method. Goodbudget is built on envelope budgeting. In its own words, it’s “a way to plan your spending instead of just tracking your spending.”
  • A real free plan. 10 regular envelopes, 10 more envelopes, 1 account, 2 devices and a year of history, free for good.
  • Budgeting together. It syncs a household budget across phones: 2 devices free, 5 on Premium.
  • Web as well as phone. It runs on the web, iPhone and Android. Vero is phone-only.
  • Debt and learning. Debt accounts track your payoff, and it offers free budgeting courses and a podcast.

If you use Goodbudget every week and it works, keep it.

If you’re typing it in anyway, make it quick

Goodbudget’s automatic bank sync is for US banks only. In the UK that makes it a manual app, the same as Vero. So the real question isn’t which app plans better. It’s which one you’ll actually keep feeding.

Goodbudget asks you to add each transaction to an envelope. Vero lets you say “coffee 3.20 and 12 for lunch at Pret” and files both. On iPhone you can do it with Siri without opening the app, and on Android Vero can offer to log your banking app’s notifications. Then streaks and challenges give you a reason to do it again tomorrow.

How Vero works instead

  • Log in one sentence. Type or say “coffee 3.20 and 12 for lunch at Pret” and Vero files both spends, with amount, place and category. You can also snap a receipt.
  • Log without opening the app. On iPhone, use Siri, the Action button or a Shortcut. On Android, Vero can offer to log the payment notifications from banking apps you choose. It reads the notification, never your bank account.
  • Use pockets like envelopes. Give money a job in pockets for rent, bills, food or a trip, from the accounts it actually sits in. Or keep it lighter with a few category caps. Budgeting is free.
  • Keep it going. Streaks, daily and weekly challenges and weekly leagues earn XP and tokens. Tokens send Verdi, Vero’s elephant, on trips that bring back discounts on gift cards from brands you already use.
  • Take your data with you. Export everything to an Excel workbook with live formulas, or your transactions to CSV.

Moving from Goodbudget

Vero doesn’t import from Goodbudget, so you start fresh. Your envelopes map across simply: add your accounts with today’s balances, make a pocket for each envelope you still use, and move money into them. Then log from today.

If you only want to try the logging habit first, skip the pockets. Cap a few categories in a plan and see if logging sticks before you rebuild everything.

Questions people ask

Is there anything Goodbudget does that Vero doesn’t?

Yes. Goodbudget has a web app as well as iPhone and Android, debt accounts for tracking payoff, budgeting courses, and automatic bank sync for US banks on Premium. Vero is phone-only and never connects to a bank.

Does Goodbudget work in the UK?

You can use it, but its automatic bank sync is for US banks only, so in the UK you add each transaction yourself. Its Premium price is listed in US dollars.

Is Goodbudget free?

Yes. The free plan includes 10 regular envelopes, 10 more envelopes, 1 account, 2 devices and 1 year of history. Premium is $10 a month or $80 a year.

Can I do envelope budgeting in Vero?

Yes. Pockets work like envelopes: you give money a job, such as rent, food or a trip, and spend from it. You can also keep it lighter with a few category caps, or mix both in one plan.

Can I move my Goodbudget envelopes to Vero?

Vero doesn’t import from Goodbudget, so you set it up fresh. Add your accounts with today’s balances, make a pocket for each envelope you still use, and move money into them.
