Vero vs Cleo

By the Vero team•Updated 24 November 2026•4 min read

We make Vero, one of the apps in this guide. Nobody paid to be included, and we’ve tried to be fair: every app gets the same questions, and we say where others do things better. Prices and features checked on 24 November 2026. Spot something out of date? Email vero.fincorp@gmail.com.

Short answer

Both apps let you manage money by chatting. The big difference is where your numbers come from.

  • Cleo connects to your bank account and talks to you about what it sees, often with a cheeky sense of humour.
  • Vero never connects to your bank. You tell Verdi what you spent, and you’re rewarded for sticking to your plan.

Pick Cleo if you want hands-off tracking from your bank feed. Pick Vero if you’d rather not share bank access and want a habit-building routine.

The biggest difference: where your numbers come from

Cleo works by connecting to your bank account, then reading your transactions. That means it can spot spending without you doing anything, and answer questions about it.

Vero takes the opposite approach. It never connects to your bank, so it only knows what you tell it. On Vero Plus, it can read the payment notifications on your phone to log spends for you.

Neither approach is “right”. Automatic tracking is less effort. Logging it yourself takes a few seconds but makes you notice each spend, and shares less with the app.

Personality and tone

Cleo is known for its personality. It can be playful and blunt, and in the UK it offers a “roast mode” that teases you about your spending if you ask for it.

Vero is gentler. Verdi, a little elephant, cheers you on and asks whether each spend was a need, a want or an impulse. The aim is to understand your habits rather than to be told off.

Rewards, challenges and pay advances

Vero is built around habits. Daily and weekly challenges, streaks and leagues with friends earn tokens. Tokens send Verdi on trips that bring back discounted gift cards. Spending more never earns you more.

Cleo’s UK app doesn’t list challenges or rewards. It does offer something Vero doesn’t: Early Income, a way to get some of your pay before payday. Check Cleo’s terms and any costs in the app before using it.

When Cleo is the better choice

  • You want tracking to happen automatically from your bank feed.
  • You enjoy a blunt, funny assistant, roast mode included.
  • You want to ask questions about past spending you never logged.
  • You’d use a feature for getting pay before payday.

When Vero is the better choice

  • You don’t want any app connected to your bank account.
  • You want a monthly plan around payday and a daily safe-to-spend number.
  • You’re motivated by streaks, challenges and real rewards.
  • You want to understand why you spend, not only what you spend.

Questions people ask

Is Cleo available in the UK?

Yes, but it’s opening up in stages. Cleo relaunched in the UK in 2026 and some users join a waitlist first. Some US features aren’t offered in the UK yet.

Does Cleo need access to my bank account?

Yes. Cleo reads your transactions through a bank connection. Vero doesn’t connect to your bank at all.

Can I use Vero and Cleo together?

You can. Some people use a bank-connected app for an overview and Vero for daily habits and challenges. Just make sure two apps don’t make you feel you’re tracking twice.

How much do they cost?

Vero is free, with Vero Plus at £4.99 a month. Cleo is free to download, and its paid Cleo Pro plan is priced in the app. We couldn’t find a published UK price.
