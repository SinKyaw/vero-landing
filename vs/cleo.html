A Cleo alternativethat never needs your bank

By the Vero team•Updated 24 November 2026•6 min read

Short answer

Cleo is good at what it does. It reads your bank account and talks to you about your spending, with a real personality. If that’s what you want, keep it.

Vero does a different job. It never connects to your bank. You log each spend in a sentence, and streaks, challenges and rewards help that become a habit.

Pick Cleo for hands-off tracking and an AI to chat with. Pick Vero if you’d rather not share bank access and want to change how you spend, not only see it.

What Cleo does well

Credit where it’s due. Cleo is one of the best-known money apps for good reasons:

  • It tracks without effort. Once your bank is connected, spending shows up by itself, including anything you’d forget to log.
  • You can chat with it. Ask about your spending in plain words and get an answer. Vero doesn’t offer an AI chat at the moment.
  • It has a personality. Cleo is playful and blunt, and in the UK it has a “roast mode” that teases you about your spending if you ask for it.
  • It can help before payday. Early Income lets you get some of your pay early. Vero has nothing like it. Check Cleo’s terms and any costs in the app first.

If those are the reasons you use Cleo, Vero won’t replace them.

Reading a feed vs building a habit

Cleo looks back. It reads your transactions after they happen and tells you what it sees. That’s useful for knowing where your money went.

Vero works at the moment you spend. There’s no bank feed, so you log each spend yourself, in one sentence. It takes a few seconds, and those seconds are the point: you notice what you’re buying while it still matters.

So the question isn’t which app tracks better. Cleo does, because it’s automatic. The question is whether you want to see your spending or change it.

How Vero works instead

  • Log in one sentence. Type or say “coffee 3.20 and 12 for lunch at Pret” and Vero files both spends, with amount, place and category. You can also snap a receipt.
  • Log without opening the app. On iPhone, use Siri, the Action button or a Shortcut. On Android, Vero can offer to log the payment notifications from banking apps you choose. It reads the notification, never your bank account.
  • Ask yourself why. A daily reflection asks whether a spend was a need, a want, an impulse or pressure, and whether you’d buy it again.
  • Keep it going. Streaks, daily and weekly challenges and weekly leagues earn XP and tokens. Tokens send Verdi, Vero’s elephant, on trips that bring back discounts on gift cards from brands you already use.
  • See if it’s working. A one-minute weekly check-in tracks how you feel about money, on a research-based wellbeing scale.

Switch, or keep both?

You don’t have to choose. Some people keep a bank-connected app for the full picture and use Vero for the daily habit. If you do, make Vero the place you log and reflect, and use Cleo to check nothing slipped through.

If you’re leaving Cleo because you’d rather no app could read your bank account, you can withdraw its access, usually in your bank’s app or in Cleo’s settings. Our guide to whether open banking is safe explains how.

Which one fits you

When Cleo is the better choice

  • You want tracking to happen automatically from your bank feed.
  • You want an AI you can ask about your spending.
  • You enjoy a blunt, funny assistant, roast mode included.
  • You’d use a feature for getting pay before payday.

When Vero is the better choice

  • You don’t want any app connected to your bank account.
  • You want to notice spending as it happens, not read about it later.
  • You’re motivated by streaks, challenges and rewards.
  • You want to understand why you spend, not only what you spend.

Questions people ask

Is there anything Cleo does that Vero doesn’t?

Yes. Cleo tracks your spending automatically from your bank, has an AI you can chat with, and offers Early Income before payday. Vero does none of these. It focuses on quick logging, reflection and habits instead.

Is Cleo available in the UK?

Yes, but it’s opening up in stages. Cleo relaunched in the UK in 2026 and some users join a waitlist first. Some US features aren’t offered in the UK yet.

Does Cleo need access to my bank account?

Yes. Cleo reads your transactions through a bank connection. Vero doesn’t connect to your bank at all.

Can Vero log my spending automatically?

Partly. Vero never connects to your bank. On Android it can log the payment notifications from banking apps you pick, and on iPhone you can log with Siri, the Action button or a Shortcut without opening the app. The free plan includes a limited number of these quick logs, and Vero Plus makes them unlimited.

How much do they cost?

Vero is free, with Vero Plus at £4.99 a month. Budgeting is the same on both. Plus adds unlimited voice and quick logging, and bigger discounts on Verdi’s trips. Cleo is free to download, and its paid Cleo Pro plan is priced in the app. We couldn’t find a published UK price.
