Budget managementin Vero

By the Vero team•Updated 27 November 2026•2 min read

Short answer

Vero gives you a few simple pieces instead of one fixed method. Accounts say where your money is. Pockets and goals say what it’s for. Plans say how much to spend and save this month. Together they work out one number: safe to spend.

Meet Mia. She’s 24, in her first proper job, and every month goes the same way. Payday lands, she feels rich for a week, and by the 20th she’s checking her bank app with one eye closed.

Mia isn’t bad with money. She just can’t see it. Her bank shows one big number, and that number doesn’t know that £750 of it is rent, £100 is for her mum, and £640 is supposed to be Thailand.

The problem with one big number

Most budgeting apps give you one method and ask you to fit into it. Some want you to sort every pound into categories. Others want you to plan every penny before you spend it. Neither fits most people for long.

Vero gives you a few simple pieces instead. Use one or all of them. Each one answers a single question about your money.

The pieces

  • Accounts answer where is it? Your current account, savings, cash and cards.
  • Pockets answer what is it for? Rent, Mum, Fun. Every pound gets a job.
  • Groups are folders that keep your pockets tidy.
  • Goals are pockets with a target and a date, like £2,000 for Thailand by March.
  • Plans answer how much this month? Spending caps, saving amounts and bills, for a month, a week or a trip.
  • Bills and IOUs track what you pay and what you’re owed.
  • Sharing lets a housemate, partner or parent see or add to a pocket or group.
  • Manage and Dashboard are two ways to see it all: everything in one grid, or views you build yourself.

The number it all adds up to

Put the pieces together and Vero can tell Mia something her bank can’t: safe to spend. That’s the money that doesn’t have a job yet, minus any bills still due that their own pockets can’t cover. For Mia, that’s £957, not £2,610. See how it’s worked out.

How to start

In the Vero app, tap Wallet in the Home header. You start by adding one account with a rough balance. There’s no bank login, and you can add the rest whenever you like.

New to budgeting? The 50/30/20 rule is a simple way to decide how big your pockets should be.

Questions people ask

Does Vero connect to my bank?

No. Vero never connects to any bank. You add your accounts with a rough balance and log spending yourself, by typing a sentence, by voice or with a receipt.

Do I have to use every piece?

No. Start with one account and a pocket or two. Groups, goals, plans, bills and sharing are there when you want them.

Is budget management free?

Yes. Budget management is the same on Vero Free and Vero Plus.
