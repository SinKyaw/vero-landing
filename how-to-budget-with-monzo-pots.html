How to budgetwith Monzo Pots

By the Vero team•Updated 24 November 2026•5 min read

Short answer

Monzo Pots let you split one balance into jobs. A simple setup that works for most people:

1. A Bills Pot that pays your Direct Debits and standing orders. 2. A Savings Pot. 3. A Pot for irregular costs like birthdays and travel. 4. Everything left in your main balance is your spending money.

Salary Sorter can move your pay into those Pots automatically every payday. All of this works on Monzo’s free account.

What Monzo Pots are

Monzo describes Pots as “separate spaces inside your Monzo account where you can set aside money for specific things.” They come free with Monzo’s current accounts, and you can have up to 20 per account.

Pots aren’t separate bank accounts. They share your account number and sort code, which is what makes moving money between them instant. Your money in Pots is protected by the FSCS in the same way as your main balance.

A simple Pots setup, step by step

1. Make a Bills Pot

Add up your rent, energy, phone and other fixed bills, and keep that amount in a Bills Pot. You can then set your Direct Debits and standing orders to come from the Pot. Monzo moves the exact amount into your main account just before each payment leaves.

Card subscriptions, like streaming or the gym, are different. To pay those from a Pot you need a virtual card linked to it, which comes with Monzo’s paid plans.

2. Make a Savings Pot

Decide what you’ll save each month and move it on payday, before you start spending. Money you don’t see in your main balance is easier to leave alone.

3. Make a Pot for irregular costs

Birthdays, trips, a new phone case, Christmas: these aren’t monthly, but they’re not surprises either. Put a little aside each month so they don’t eat into a single month’s spending.

4. Leave your spending money in the main balance

Once bills, savings and irregular costs are in Pots, whatever is left in your main balance is what you can spend until next payday. That one number does a lot of the work.

Automate it on payday

Salary Sorter moves your salary or student loan into Pots as soon as it arrives, and remembers your split for next time. Monzo says it works for UK residents aged 16 and over, on payments over £100.

Get Paid Early means a salary or student loan paid by Bacs can reach your Monzo account from 4pm one business day early.

What Pots don’t do

Pots are brilliant at keeping money apart. They answer “where is my money?”. What they don’t do is ask about the spending itself: whether that £12 lunch was a need or an impulse, or whether you’d buy it again.

That gap is where most budgets wobble. The bills are covered, the savings are moved, and the main balance still drains faster than planned, one small spend at a time.

Questions people ask

Are Monzo Pots free?

Yes. Pots come with Monzo’s free current account, as well as joint, 16-17 and under-16s accounts. You can have up to 20 Pots per account.

Are Pots separate bank accounts?

No. Pots are spaces inside your Monzo account and share its account number and sort code.

Can bills come straight out of a Pot?

Yes. You can set Direct Debits and standing orders to come from a Pot. Card payments, such as streaming subscriptions, need a virtual card linked to the Pot, which comes with Monzo’s paid plans.

Does Salary Sorter work with a student loan?

Yes. Monzo says Salary Sorter can split your salary or your student loan into Pots. It works on payments over £100.

Does Vero connect to Monzo?

No. Vero never connects to any bank. You log spending yourself, by a sentence, voice or receipt. On Android, Vero can offer to log the payment notifications from the Monzo app.
