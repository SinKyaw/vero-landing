Groups: folders foryour pockets

By the Vero team•Updated 27 November 2026•1 min read

Short answer

A group is an optional folder for pockets, with the group’s total on top. Groups don’t change how money works; they just make it easier to see. A pocket can also stay unfiled.

Once Mia started using pockets, she couldn’t stop. Rent, Mum, Fun, Concert, Thailand, and next month there’ll be a Christmas pocket and one for her sister’s birthday. Scrolling a long list to find the right one got old fast.

The problem

A long flat list hides the shape of your money. Mia wants to know “how much is set aside for home?” or “how much is for me?” without adding up pockets in her head.

How groups work

A group is a folder for pockets. Give it a name, put pockets in it, and Vero shows the group’s total on top. Mia uses three:

  • Home: Rent
  • Family: Mum
  • Me: Fun, Concert and her Thailand goal

Set one up

  1. In budget management, open Groups under More, and tap +.
  2. Give it a name, like Home or Thailand trip.
  3. Pick the pockets that belong in it. You can move a pocket to another group at any time.

Use it for

  • Life areas: Home, Me, Family
  • Big events: a Wedding group with Venue, Outfit and Gifts pockets
  • A trip: Flights, Stays and Spending money
  • Shared costs: a Flat group you share with housemates (see Sharing)
  • A plan: cap a whole group at once instead of each pocket

Good to know

  • Delete a group and its pockets simply become unfiled. No money moves.
  • Moving a pocket into a shared group changes who can see it. Vero asks before it does.
  • When you first open budget management, Vero suggests a few example groups. Rename or remove them.

Questions people ask

Do I have to use groups?

No. Groups are optional. Pockets and goals can stay unfiled.

What happens to pockets when I delete a group?

They become unfiled. Their money stays where it is.
