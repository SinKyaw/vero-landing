Pockets: give everypound a job

By the Vero team•Updated 27 November 2026•2 min read

Short answer

A pocket is a named pot for a purpose: Rent, Mum, Fun. You fill it with Move money from money that has no job yet. It isn’t spending and it isn’t income, so your totals stay honest. Nothing moves at your bank.

Mia’s money used to sit in one heap. Rent, money for her mum, and the fund for a festival ticket all looked the same in her bank app. So the festival fund quietly turned into takeaways.

The problem

Spending categories only tell you what money went on. They can’t tell you what money is for, or whose it is. “Money for Mum”, “rent” and “Glastonbury” end up flattened into one balance until it’s too late.

How pockets work

A pocket is a named pot for a purpose. Mia has five: Rent, Mum, Fun, Concert and her Thailand goal. Each pocket shows how much it holds and which of her accounts that money sits in.

To fill a pocket she uses Move money: from her Vero Wallet (money with no job yet) into the pocket. It’s like a transfer between pots. It isn’t spending and it isn’t income. And Vero won’t let her move more than is safe to spend.

Mia moves £85 into Concert. Her safe to spend drops from £957 to £872, because that money now has a job.

Use it for

  • Bills: a Rent pocket that fills up through the month
  • People: money for Mum, a sibling or a pet
  • Sinking funds: birthdays, car insurance, a new phone
  • Fun money: a Fun pocket that’s guilt-free until it’s empty
  • Money someone owes you: a pocket can start “in the red” if you paid for something first

Good to know

  • Pockets label money inside your real accounts. They aren’t new bank accounts.
  • Spent from the wrong account? That’s allowed. The pocket offers to square it up for you.
  • Delete a pocket and its money goes straight back to your wallet. Nothing gets lost.
  • Each pocket can have its own currency, which helps if you’re saving for a trip abroad.

Questions people ask

Are pockets separate bank accounts?

No. Pockets label money that sits in your real accounts. Nothing moves at your bank.

Is moving money into a pocket counted as spending?

No. Moving money into a pocket isn’t spending or income, so your totals stay accurate. You can only move money that’s safe to spend.

What happens when I delete a pocket?

Its money goes back to your Vero Wallet in the same step, so nothing is lost.
