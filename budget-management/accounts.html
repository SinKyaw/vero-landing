Accounts andsafe to spend

By the Vero team•Updated 27 November 2026•2 min read

Short answer

Add your real accounts once, with a rough balance. Vero then works out safe to spend: what’s in your accounts, minus money already given a job in pockets and goals, minus bills still due that their own pocket can’t cover.

Mia has three places her money lives: a current account with £1,450, a savings account with £1,100 and £60 in cash. That’s £2,610.

It feels like a lot. It isn’t. Rent leaves on the 1st, her mum gets £100 a month, and £640 of the savings is for Thailand. The real question is never how much do I have? It’s how much can I spend without breaking something?

The problem

Your bank shows a balance. It doesn’t know which of that money is already spoken for. So you either spend it and panic later, or you do mental maths every time you tap your card.

How accounts work

Add each real account once: name, type and roughly what’s in it. Bank, savings and cash count as money you have. Credit cards count as money you owe. Vero never connects to your bank, so a rough balance is fine, and you keep it up to date by logging as you go.

How safe to spend is worked out

Vero starts from what’s in your accounts, then takes away:

  • money you’ve already given a job in pockets and goals
  • bills still due that their own pocket can’t cover

What’s left is safe to spend. Mia’s rent is due too, but her Rent pocket already holds it, so it isn’t counted twice.

The Vero Wallet

Safe to spend sits at the top of budget management, on a stack of your account cards called the Vero Wallet. Tap it to show a different headline: all accounts, one account, one group or one pocket. The eye button hides every amount and remembers it, which helps on the bus.

Use it for

  • Checking whether you can say yes to Friday plans
  • Keeping an emergency fund out of the total (an account can be left out)
  • Seeing your credit card as debt, not as spending money

Good to know

  • Balances are yours to keep right. There’s no bank sync, which also means no bank login. Read is open banking safe? if you’re weighing that up.
  • Each account can have its own currency, and totals convert to your preferred one.

Questions people ask

What does safe to spend mean in Vero?

It’s the money in your accounts that doesn’t have a job yet, minus any bills still due that their own pocket can’t cover. It isn’t a daily allowance; it’s what you can spend without touching money set aside for something else.

Do I need exact balances?

No. A rough balance is enough to start. You keep it close by logging what you spend and earn.

Can I leave an account out?

Yes. An account can be left out of the total, for example an emergency fund you don’t want to count as spending money.
