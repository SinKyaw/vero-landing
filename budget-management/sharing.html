Sharing: managemoney together

By the Vero team•Updated 27 November 2026•2 min read

Short answer

Share a single pocket or a whole group by invite or link. Each person can view or can add spending. Only the owner decides how much the pockets hold, and nobody sees which bank accounts the money sits in.

Mia lives in Flat 4B with Sam and Jo. Every month there’s the same muddle: who topped up the electric, who bought the loo roll, whose turn it is to pay for broadband. Nobody wants a joint bank account. Everybody wants to stop doing sums in the group chat.

The problem

Money you manage with or for someone is invisible to them. A partner, a housemate or a parent can’t see what’s in the pot or what’s been spent, so every question turns into a message.

How sharing works

Share a single pocket or a whole group. Invite someone in the app, or send a link that lasts 7 days. Mia makes a Flat 4B group with three pockets and shares it: Sam can add spending, Jo can view.

What each role can do in a shared pocket or group
Can viewCan add
See balances, pockets and historyYesYes
Record spendingNoYes
Add pockets in a shared groupNoYes
Change how much a pocket holdsNoNo, only the owner

What stays private

  • Members never see which bank accounts the money sits in.
  • Every transaction stays owned by whoever logged it.
  • An activity timeline shows who joined, left or changed something.

Use it for

  • Housemates: shared bills and household bits
  • Couples: a date-night or holiday pocket
  • Family: pocket money for a younger sibling, or money you manage for a parent

Good to know

  • If they don’t have Vero yet, the invite still works after they install it.
  • Moving a pocket into or out of a shared group changes who can see it, so Vero asks first.
  • There are no per-person spending limits or joint plans yet.
  • For one-off debts like “you owe me £40”, use a shared IOU instead.

Questions people ask

Do I need a joint bank account to share a budget?

No. Sharing in Vero works on pockets and groups, not bank accounts, and members never see which accounts the money sits in.

Can someone I share with take money out of a pocket?

No. People who can add record spending, but only the owner decides how much a pocket holds.

How long does an invite link last?

Seven days.
