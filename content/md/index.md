# Vero: Gamified Budgeting App, No Open Banking Needed

> Plan your month, log spending in seconds and earn real rewards with Verdi, your elephant mate.

Vero is a mobile app for iOS and Android, made by Vero Financial Ltd. It does not connect to or access your bank account. Free to start, with an optional Vero Plus plan for £4.99 a month.

## Why Vero

Vero makes budgeting fun, stress-free and rewarding.

- **Money challenges that build better habits.** Saving challenges help you keep more of your money.
- **Seamless tracking.** Auto-tracking, typing or voice logging. Your spending, logged your way.
- **Know why you spent.** Reflect on each spend as a need, want or impulse.
- **Manage money your way.** Plan, set money aside in pockets and know exactly where you stand.
- **A companion who rewards you.** Earn rewards from Verdi: pre-paid, discounted gift cards from brands you love.

## How Vero works

1. **Plan your month.** Set a monthly plan that matches your payday, put money aside in pockets for rent and bills, and see a safe-to-spend number every day.
2. **Log your expenses.** Text Verdi like a friend, use voice logging, or let Vero Plus auto-track. Tag each spend as need, want or impulse.
3. **Earn tokens.** Complete daily and weekly money challenges, build streaks and compete in weekly leagues with friends.
4. **Send Verdi on a trip.** Spend tokens to fund Verdi's trip. He comes back with discounted gift cards and the odd surprise gift.

## Pricing

## FAQ

**Is my data safe?** Yes. Vero doesn't connect to or access your bank account, and your data stays private.

**Is Vero free?** Yes. Vero is free to use, with an optional Vero Plus plan for £4.99 a month.

**What can Vero tokens do?** You earn tokens from money challenges and use them to fund Verdi's trips. When he comes back, he brings rewards with him.

**Do rewards encourage spending?** No. You earn tokens for tracking, sticking to your budget and finishing challenges. Spending more never earns you more.

**How do I redeem rewards?** When Verdi brings back a reward, open it in the app and follow the steps to claim your discount at the partner brand.

**How does auto-tracking work without open banking?** Automated logging (Vero Plus) reads your payment notifications, so it works while notifications are on. On iPhone you set it up once in Shortcuts; on Android you allow it in the Vero app. You can also type or speak what you spent.

**Can Vero help me save money?** Yes. Your monthly plan, pockets, money challenges and spending reflections help you spend on purpose and cut what you don't need.

**Who is Verdi?** Verdi is your elephant mate in the app. He cheers you on, goes on trips funded by your tokens and comes back with rewards. https://verofin.uk/verdi-companion
