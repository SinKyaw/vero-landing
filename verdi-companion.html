Introducing our most‑awaited changes!

If you’ve been with us for a while, you’ll remember the old setup: complete money challenges, earn tokens, and swap them for gift cards.

But we wanted your habits to be the point, not just the thing you did to earn rewards.

So, meet Verdi — a little elephant who grows as your money habits do.

Your tokens now go into Verdi’s journey fund.

Keep tracking, stick to your spending plan, and complete challenges to fill it up. Once it’s ready, Verdi heads off on an adventure!

And what does he bring back? A trunk full of rewards — from everyday essentials to the fun stuff, with discounts at brands you already love.

Not ready to use a reward yet? Pop it in the Vault.

Rewards Verdi brings back need claiming within 24 hours. If you’re not ready to use one yet, save it to the Vault and come back to it when you need it.

His hearts reflect how your money habits are going.

Keep tracking and stay within your spending plan to keep him adventure-ready. Miss a few days? He’ll slow down, but he won’t hold it against you.

Savings shows what you’ve kept in your pocket.

Every discount Verdi brings home adds up. Your piggy bank tracks how much you’ve saved through his rewards, so you can see your good habits paying off.

And the best part?

There’s no more cap on rewards! They work a little differently now, so send Verdi on his first adventure and see for yourself.

Your previously earned tokens are safe and sound, ready to fund his first trip.
