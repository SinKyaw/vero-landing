Meet Verdi, your money habits buddy

Verdi is a little elephant who grows as your money habits do.

Your tokens go into Verdi’s journey fund.

Keep tracking, stick to your spending plan, and complete challenges to earn tokens. Once the fund is ready, Verdi heads off on an adventure!

Every adventure ends with a trunk of rewards, from everyday essentials to the fun stuff. Instead of a straight gift card, Verdi brings back discounts at brands you already shop with. They come in two forms:

  • Unique discount codes from Vero’s brand friends
  • Discounted gift cards through our official partner, Spendstream

Not ready to use a reward yet? Pop it in the Vault.

The trunk stays open for 12 hours, so claim what you want in that time. If you’re not ready to use a reward yet, save it to the Vault and come back to it when you need it. Free users can store one reward, and Plus users can keep three.

Savings shows what you’ve kept in your pocket.

Say Verdi comes back with 3.5% off at a coffee shop. You buy a £15 gift card for £14.48, and the 52p you saved lands in Verdi’s piggy bank. It might be 50p one day and a couple of pounds the next, but you’ll watch it add up and see your good habits paying off.

And the best part?

There’s no cap on rewards!

To send Verdi on his first adventure, you simply need a tracking streak of at least 3 days.

So stay consistent, send Verdi on his first journey and see for yourself.
